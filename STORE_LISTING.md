## Detailed description (shown on the listing page)

Stop retyping the same information over and over.

Autofill Vault lets you save the info you enter into forms all the time —
your name, address, phone number, email, LinkedIn profile, even payment
card numbers — and fill it into any field on any website with a drag or a
click.

**How it works**
1. Click the extension icon and add your saved items
2. Hover the small lock icon that appears on any page to see your saved
   chips
3. Drag a chip onto a field, or click a field then click a chip, to fill it
   instantly

**Built with privacy first**
- Everything is stored locally on your device — nothing is ever sent to a
  server, ours or anyone else's
- No account, no sign-up, no tracking
- Sensitive items (like card numbers) are protected with AES-256 encryption
  behind a passcode that's never stored anywhere

**Why you'll want this**
- Job applications: stop retyping your address and phone number for the
  hundredth time
- Online shopping: fill checkout forms in seconds
- Signups and surveys: get through repetitive forms faster

Free to use. No account required. Your data never leaves your browser.
